Dino Gang

Little learning games for ages 3 to 6, played with a grown-up. Free, no accounts, no ads. Works on a phone, tablet or computer, and offline after the first visit.

What's inside

Five places to play. Each wizard has its own pompom jar.

Word Wizards

Phonics sound cards. Autumn 1 (20 sounds) and Autumn 2 (15 sounds) are ready, with Spring 1, other spellings and tricky words to come. Press Sound to hear the pure sound ("sss", not "suh"), then the word.

Number Wizards

Counting to 5 and to 10, spotting each number as a coloured block pattern, showing it on friendly hands (one hand to 5, two hands to 10), and number bonds to 5 and 10. Point to each object once and count out loud.

Spanish Wizards

First Spanish (Spain): greetings, colours, numbers 1 to 10 and short phrases. A picture or a short clip, a question you hear, then the answer.

Portuguese Wizards

The same four sets in European Portuguese (Portugal).

🌍

All words (in Language Wizards)

Just listening, no scores. Tap a picture to hear the English word, then Spanish, Portuguese or both. Choose with the flags at the top.

Playing a card

One short session is ten cards.

  1. Pick a setTap a tile on the home screen.
  2. ListenPress the sound button. Language cards play the question on their own.
  3. Turn overTap the card or the blue Turn over button. The answer shows, and Language cards say it.
  4. Got it or Not yetBe honest. Both earn a pompom.

With Word first, the same sound shows different pictures: a sun, a seal, a snake. Picture cards are silent unless you switch on "Say it aloud automatically" in the settings, so your child can name the picture first. Tap "Hear the word" or "Sound" whenever you want to hear it.

Standard picture words, or more. Each sound starts with one familiar picture word. Choose "More picture words" to mix in extra pictures for variety. Your choice is remembered.

Pompoms

Every card finished earns a pompom, so effort is rewarded, not just right answers. Each wizard has its own pompom total at the top of the screen, and the jars on the home screen fill as cards are learned. There are no streaks, hearts or leaderboards.

How it fits school

Phonics

Word Wizards teaches the letter sounds in a sequence based on the Department for Education's Letters and Sounds guidance (2007), the framework behind many school phonics programmes. It starts with the first 20 sounds, then the next 15. Sounds are said in the clipped way schools teach them, with a picture word to go with each.

Early maths

Number Wizards covers the Reception Number ideas: counting objects one to one, knowing that the last number says how many, recognising numerals, and seeing a number as two parts. It is not tied to one maths scheme.

Languages

Foreign languages are only required from Key Stage 2, so Spanish and Portuguese here are early listening and fun: hear a word, say it back, link it to a picture. Spain Spanish and Portugal Portuguese.

How cards are learned

A card counts as learned after three "Got it" taps in a row. "Not yet" brings the card back soon. There are no wrong answers and no timers.

Ages: 3 to 4, listen and point. 5 to 6, say it. A grown-up beside the child works best.

Menus and settings

  • Logo, top left: back to the list of wizards.
  • Menu, top right: switch between Word Wizards, Number Wizards and Language Wizards. Language Wizards opens Spanish, Portuguese and All words.
  • Cog, top right: for grown-ups, so press and hold for one second. It opens the settings.

In the settings:

  • Colours: Light (the standard), Dark, or match the device.
  • Say it aloud automatically: plays the sound when a card turns over, and on picture cards the word when the card appears and the sound and word when it turns over. Off by default.
  • Language questions: a mix, "what does it mean?" (listening), or "how do you say it?" (speaking).
  • Clear progress: tap twice. This wipes pompoms and learned cards on this device.

Good to know

  • Add to Home Screen: on iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the Chrome menu, then Install app.
  • Offline: open it once online, then it works without internet.
  • Updates: new versions download quietly in the background. Close the app and open it again to see them. The "What's new" note on the home screen says what changed.
  • Private: no accounts or tracking. Progress stays on the device, so it does not move between devices.
