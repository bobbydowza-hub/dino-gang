# EL question-piece format (Tom's reworked version, 2026-10-20)

Tom reworked the question pieces to get the right enunciation. Use this approach for question pieces going forward (not the plain `[energetic] <line>` form in `docs/el-questions.txt`).

What changed:
- Each piece is said with a stand-in word (THIS, Ola, Goodbye, "Bonjour") inside it, so the intonation lands naturally. The stand-in word must be cut out of the final clip (the piece should be only the opening or ending part). TO CONFIRM with Tom how he wants the cut: remove the stand-in word, or he trims in GarageBand.
- Pauses inside the line: `[pause]` and `...` around the stand-in word.
- One line uses `[excited]` instead of `[energetic]` ("What does [pause]... THIS mean?").
- Four pauses at the end, then `...` blocks with blank lines between clips, as before.
